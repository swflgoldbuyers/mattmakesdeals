Collier County

Naples

Southwest Florida’s most established coastal destination — Fifth Avenue South, the beaches, and some of the region’s most recognized residential communities.

Naples is the Collier County market most people outside the region already know. Beaches, downtown, and a long-standing reputation as a coastal destination give it a different weight than the Lee County cities to the north.

That reputation does not make Naples simple. It is a collection of neighborhoods, beach corridors, and inland communities, each with its own character. Understanding Naples means understanding how it sits next to Bonita Springs and the rest of Southwest Florida — not treating it as an island.

Matt Makes Deals covers Naples as part of a four-market Southwest Florida map. The point is not to flatten the city into a luxury label. The point is to read it clearly, alongside Fort Myers, Estero, and Bonita Springs.

Naples is the Collier County market in the Matt Makes Deals coverage area, south of Bonita Springs.
