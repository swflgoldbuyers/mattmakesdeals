Bonita Springs

A quieter Gulf-adjacent city between Estero and Naples — Imperial River, nearby beaches, and a mix of older neighborhoods and newer communities.

Bonita Springs sits on the southern edge of Lee County, between Estero and the Collier County line. It has Gulf access, the Imperial River, and a pace that often feels different from both Fort Myers and Naples.

The market mixes older Florida neighborhoods with newer residential communities, plus a downtown river district and beaches a short drive west. People looking here are often trying to stay close to the coast without automatically defaulting to Naples.

Matt Makes Deals covers Bonita Springs because the city is easy to skip on a map and hard to skip in real life. It is part of how Southwest Florida actually lives, not just how it is marketed.

Bonita Springs is the southern Lee County market in the Matt Makes Deals coverage area, between Estero and Naples.
