Estero

Between Fort Myers and Naples. Coconut Point, Florida Gulf Coast University, and master-planned community living along one of the region’s main corridors.

Estero occupies the stretch of Lee County between Fort Myers and Bonita Springs. It is not a downtown river city and it is not a Gulf beach town. It is a corridor market — I-75, Coconut Point, Florida Gulf Coast University — with a large share of master-planned community living.

That position matters. People looking at Estero are often comparing it to Fort Myers to the north and Bonita Springs or Naples to the south. The questions are usually about lifestyle, commute, and which version of Southwest Florida they actually want.

Matt Makes Deals treats Estero as its own market, not a footnote between two better-known names. Understanding Estero is part of understanding how Southwest Florida is growing.

Estero sits between Fort Myers and Bonita Springs, and it is one of four markets covered by Matt Makes Deals.
