Three series

A real estate and investing desk, not a listing feed.

Current notes live on social. This page is the structure of the publication — what gets covered, how often, and why it exists.

Weekly Blog Every Monday

Notes from the market

Short, useful writing on real estate, investing, and business in Southwest Florida. The Monday notes are for people who want a clearer read on Fort Myers, Estero, Bonita Springs, and Naples — not a recap of national headlines.

Follow along on Instagram, X, and LinkedIn as new notes go out.

Monthly Market Reports

Southwest Florida market reports

A slower look at local housing context across the four markets. These reports are meant to help buyers, sellers, and investors see the region as a set of places — not a single statewide story.

Ongoing Investment Mindset

How investors think

Writing about risk, timing, and long-term wealth building. The point is not to sell a formula. It is to show how thoughtful investors look at Southwest Florida real estate before they move.

The four-market map

Every insight is written against a specific geography: Fort Myers, Estero, Bonita Springs, and Naples. If a note is about Southwest Florida, it should be able to name the place.
